rclone-zygalski

This is my personal rclone configuration for mounting Google Drive on my Linux computer. It lets me access and manage files made available to the app through Google Drive. It is not a service offered to other users.

The app requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to provide the mount and perform file operations. See the privacy policy for how the app accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google data.
